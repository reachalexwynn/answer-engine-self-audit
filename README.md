# Answer-Engine Self-Audit

A free, self-serve checklist for one question: **when a buyer asks ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity what to use, why do they recommend your competitor and not you?**

Run this in about an hour. No tools required beyond a browser and accounts on the answer engines.

## Step 1 - Collect the questions your buyers actually ask (10 min)

Pull 10-20 real pre-purchase questions from sales calls, support tickets, search console, and Reddit threads in your category. Examples: "best X for Y", "X vs Y", "is X worth it for [use case]", "X alternatives".

## Step 2 - Run every question through all four engines (20 min)

Ask each question verbatim in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity, in fresh logged-out sessions where possible. For each answer, record:

- [ ] Are you mentioned at all?
- [ ] Are you recommended, or just listed?
- [ ] Which competitors are named, and in what order?
- [ ] Which sources are cited or linked (Perplexity and Gemini show these explicitly)?

## Step 3 - Trace every recommendation back to its evidence (15 min)

For each competitor that beats you, open the cited sources. You will almost always find one or more of:

- [ ] A third-party "best of" listicle that includes them and not you
- [ ] A comparison page on their own site targeting "[you] vs [them]" or "[them] alternatives"
- [ ] Active, recent threads on Reddit or forums mentioning them by name
- [ ] A Wikipedia, G2, Capterra, or review-site entry that is filled out and current
- [ ] Documentation or pricing pages that directly answer the question in plain text

## Step 4 - Score the gaps (10 min)

- [ ] **Mention gap**: sources cite competitors where you are absent entirely
- [ ] **Evidence gap**: your own site lacks a page that directly answers the buyer question
- [ ] **Freshness gap**: competitor mentions are recent; yours are old or stale
- [ ] **Consensus gap**: multiple independent sources agree on the competitor, which the models weight heavily

## Step 5 - Fix in order of leverage

1. Create or update the one page on your site that directly answers your highest-value buyer question in plain, citable text.
2. Get included in the 2-3 third-party listicles the engines already cite for your category (most accept outreach or submissions).
3. Close the review-site gaps: complete and update your G2 / Capterra / category-directory profiles.
4. Publish one honest "us vs them" comparison page for your closest competitor.

Re-run Step 2 monthly. Answers shift as sources change.
